Underwriting Blueprint

The 6-Pillar Alaya Advisory Framework

A systematic approach to evaluating real estate opportunities, mitigating legal and zoning risks, and structuring high-conviction portfolios.

01

Market Intelligence

Macro-economic growth vectors overlaid with micro-market pricing trends. We track land absorption rates, absorption velocities, and infrastructure timelines before assets price in.

Metric: Growth Rate & Absorption Index
02

Location & Corridor Analysis

Granular assessment of master plans, upcoming expressway junctions, bypass links, and municipal zoning modifications to identify asymmetric appreciation vectors.

Metric: 5-Yr Corridor Development Plan
03

Pricing & Comparables

Rigorous comparative market analysis utilizing verified government registry data and unlisted transaction registries to establish true intrinsic value without sentiment premium.

Metric: Net Registry vs. Transacted Cap
04

30-Year Title Due Diligence

Exhaustive legal auditing: 30-year revenue search, pedigree mutation trace, physical possession checks, town & country planning approvals, and environmental encumbrance scans.

Metric: 100% Clear Title Verification
05

Financial Modeling

Stress-tested discounted cash flow (DCF) simulations, cap rate sensitivity models, yield distribution projections, and debt structuring optimization to guarantee resilient IRR.

Metric: Levered & Unlevered IRR (12-18%)
06

Exit & Liquidity Strategy

Every acquisition is structured with pre-planned liquidity mechanisms: staged secondary retail parceling, corporate buyouts, or long-term lease securitization.

Metric: 3 to 7 Year Liquidity Roadmap
