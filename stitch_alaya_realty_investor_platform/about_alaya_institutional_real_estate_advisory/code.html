About Alaya Realty

Experience. Perspective. Accountability.

We are an institutionally minded real estate advisory firm serving discerning investors and families. Our approach is defined by rigor, transparency, and an unwavering commitment to client outcomes.

Rooted in Excellence, Global in Perspective.

Founded in Indore, Alaya Realty emerged from a fundamental need: to bring institutional-grade advisory and operational transparency to the private real estate market. We recognized that significant transactions require more than just facilitation; they require strategic foresight.

Our journey has been defined by advising on complex acquisitions, structuring generational portfolios, and consistently prioritizing long-term value creation over transactional volume.

Our Core Convictions

01

Uncompromising Trust

Trust is not granted; it is architected through consistency. We operate as fiduciaries, placing client interests unequivocally first in every negotiation and strategic recommendation.

The Alaya Standard

Absolute Transparency

In an opaque market, clarity is a competitive advantage. We provide unvarnished insights, rigorous diligence, and transparent fee structures. We believe that informed capital is secure capital.

Our advisory is backed by quantitative depth. We do not rely on market sentiment; we rely on data-driven underwriting, comprehensive market analysis, and meticulous risk assessment.

The Senior-Led Advantage

At Alaya, clients do not interface with junior associates. Every relationship, analysis, and negotiation is directly managed by a senior principal. This structural commitment ensures that our highest level of intellectual capital is applied to every mandate, minimizing execution risk and maximizing strategic impact.
