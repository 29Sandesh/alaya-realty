Our story

Quiet,
thorough,
measured.

Alaya Realty was built on a simple conviction — that real estate advice should come from the people actually doing the work, not the names on the deck.

We work with buyers, investors, occupiers, and developers across residential, office, retail, and industrial assets. Every brief is led end-to-end by a founding partner, supported by a team that knows the ground.

Our work is quiet, thorough, and measured by the quality of decisions it helps clients make — not the volume of deals done.

Core Ethos

Fiduciary Commitment

Every mandate receives senior-level analytical rigor, deep local market intelligence, and hands-on execution.

Principles

What we believe

Four principles, written down so we don't drift from them.

01

Senior-led, always

A partner leads every mandate end-to-end. You never speak to the deck, you speak to the decision-maker.

02

Discipline over volume

We take on what we can execute well. Fewer mandates, more attention, better outcomes.

03

A point of view

We don't hedge. Clients hire us for conviction, not a balanced menu of possibilities.

04

Local hands, deep lens

Our advisors have worked Indore's submarkets for years — close enough to the ground to know where price lives on any given day.
